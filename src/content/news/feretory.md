# Development Spotlight: The Feretory

**A new path to progression, discovery, and adventure.**

In Cleopatra XI, character progression is about more than gaining levels and collecting equipment. The **Feretory** introduces another way to grow: pursue challenges, earn a special currency called **Infamy**, and exchange it for meaningful, permanent character unlocks.

Whether you're building a more versatile Trust party, working toward experience-related benefits, or preparing for legendary endgame regions, the Feretory gives you new goals to pursue alongside the classic level 75 experience.

## What Is the Feretory?

The Feretory is Cleopatra XI's custom progression and reward system. It connects challenges and unlocks through a dedicated shop where players can spend Infamy on new capabilities and opportunities.

The goal isn't to replace Vana'diel's missions, jobs, or equipment progression. It's to give adventurers **more ways to shape their journey** and more reasons to explore the world.

## Challenges and Infamy

**Infamy** is the currency used to obtain Feretory rewards.

The system features **14 monster families** associated with its progression trials. These challenges provide goals beyond ordinary leveling, while some unlocks also carry minimum level requirements.

As the system develops, we'll share the specific trial objectives, Infamy awards, and shop costs so players can plan their progression.

## What Can You Unlock?

The Feretory reward shop brings several types of character progression together in one place.

### Trust Companions and Support Jobs

One of the Feretory's biggest roles is expanding what players can do with Cleopatra XI's customized Trust system.

Planned reward categories include:

- **Fellowship** — A foundational Trust-related unlock.
- **Warrior's Tome** — Unlocks Warrior subjob customization.
- **Red Mage's Tome** — Unlocks Red Mage subjob customization.
- **Samurai's Tome** — Unlocks Samurai subjob customization.
- **Ninja's Tome** — Unlocks Ninja subjob customization.
- **Trust Expansion I and II** — Increase the number of Trust companions you can summon.
- **Mastery** — Unlocks access to advanced Trust ciphers.

Support jobs aren't one-size-fits-all. Each Trust has its own eligible configurations, and a different support job can change the abilities, priorities, or combat role available to that companion.

For more details, visit [The Cleopatra Trust System](/wiki/trust-system/) and explore the [Trust Compendium](/wiki/trusts/).

### Regional Writs

The Feretory also includes **regional writs** as part of its progression rewards. We'll publish the specific benefits and requirements for each writ as those details are finalized.

### Experience-Related Rewards

Two additional unlocks, **Tome of Experience I** and **Tome of Experience II**, are designed to provide experience-related progression benefits. Exact bonuses and requirements will be announced when finalized.

## Unlocking Vana'diel's Legendary Regions

**Your journey. Your choice. Your path to endgame.**

One of the Feretory's most significant features is the ability to unlock access to two of Final Fantasy XI's iconic regions: **Tu'Lia (Sky)** and **Al'Taieu (Sea)**.

Traditionally, reaching these destinations requires advancing through their respective expansion mission storylines. In Cleopatra XI, the Feretory offers another route: **spend Infamy on the appropriate unlock to enter the region without completing the missions normally required for access.**

### Tu'Lia — Sky

High above Vana'diel lies **Tu'Lia**, the celestial realm adventurers know as **Sky**. Normally reached through *Rise of the Zilart* mission progression, Sky is a defining destination of the level 75 era.

With the Feretory's Tu'Lia unlock, players can gain access to Sky without first completing the required Zilart missions.

### Al'Taieu — Sea

**Al'Taieu**, commonly called **Sea**, is the mysterious region associated with *Chains of Promathia*.

The Feretory's Al'Taieu unlock allows players to enter Sea without first completing the missions normally required to reach it.

### But the Stories Still Matter

**Unlocking an area is not the same as completing its story.**

Feretory access opens the door to Sky or Sea, but **it does not award the unique rewards earned by completing the corresponding expansion storyline**.

Those storylines remain valuable in Cleopatra XI. Players who follow the missions through to their conclusions will receive **unique completion rewards**, giving adventurers a meaningful reason to experience the original stories even if they've already purchased area access.

You can use Infamy to reach an endgame region sooner and still return to finish its missions later. Neither path closes off the other.

**The Feretory opens the doors. The stories hold rewards of their own.**

*Availability of specific endgame encounters follows Cleopatra XI's content release schedule.*

## Permanent, Character-Wide Progression

Feretory unlocks are **permanent for the character that earns them**. Once that character obtains a permanent reward, the unlock remains part of their progression rather than expiring at the end of a session.

These unlocks are **not account-wide**: each character develops its own Feretory progression and obtains its own rewards.

That makes every unlock an achievement tied to the adventurer who earned it.

## A System Built to Grow

The Feretory provides a foundation for ongoing Cleopatra XI progression. Its initial focus includes monster-family trials, Infamy, Trust customization, and permanent unlocks, with room for additional rewards as new content becomes available.

Our aim is to preserve what makes classic Vana'diel memorable while offering players more flexibility in how they experience it.

Some adventurers will prioritize their Trust companions. Others will pursue experience-related benefits or work toward earlier access to Sky and Sea. And those who want to see the original stories through will still have unique rewards waiting for them.

**Welcome to the Feretory. Your achievements are your currency.**
